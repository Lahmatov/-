Walk every Trello list: fix wrong statuses, unstick stalled cards (ask the executor or their manager),
update milestone progress in "🎯 Цели и этапы", make sure every board decision the agents are waiting
for is in "👤 Нужно решение владельца" with options and a recommendation. Post a summary comment on
the M-card of the current milestone: done this week, top three next, blockers, decisions needed.

Runs when the owner asks ("прогони продюсера") or on a schedule the owner has approved.
